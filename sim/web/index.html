Supply the required measurements to calculate a resting waterline. The centre plane is a reference, not a predicted water surface.

01 / Build & water

+X forward · +Y port · +Z up. Positions in metres from the labelled assembly centre. CAD-derived centres are allowed only for uniform wooden parts.

02 / Mass, balance & displacement

Weigh each group including its listed hardware. Each physical item belongs to exactly one group. Untick absent groups.

03 / Extra loads & ballast

Use for equipment or ballast not already included in a group mass. For enclosed loads, name the enclosing component. Exposed boxes need measured dimensions.

04 / Water trials

Signed millimetres: + above centre, − below. Read the undisturbed surface, avoiding the meniscus. Front and rear alone do not determine roll. Give every loading a unique configuration name.
