Sailing & turning

          

          

            How a run works. The turtle starts at the green dot, still in
            the water, with its bow pointing the way you set. Wind and current
            stay steady for the whole run. Nobody steers: the only control is
            the sail, set with the dials below. Heading, speed and drift all
            come from the forces, so the turtle usually turns away from its
            start heading within seconds.
          

          
          

            Pick a starting point, then change anything: as soon as you touch a
            dial or field the scenario becomes Custom.
          

          

          

            Drag a dial, or click it and use the arrow keys (Shift for 1°
            steps). Directions are compass degrees: 0 north, 90 east, 180 south,
            270 west.
          

          

            

              Bow points at start
              

              Where the turtle faces at the start. It then turns
                freely.
            

            

              Wind from
              

              
              Where the wind comes from, as in a weather report. 180 = from
                the south, blowing north.
            

            

              Current from
              

              
              Where the water comes from. 0 = from the north, flowing
                south.
            

            
          

          

            Sail and timing. The sail is the turtle's only control. Set
            where it starts, and optionally one change partway through the trip.
            Sail angles are measured from the hull: 0° = square across (catches
            wind from behind). The grey arc is beyond the servo's reach.
          

          

            

              Sail at start
              

              Sail angle to the hull when the trip begins.
            

            

              Change sail after
              

              Minutes into the trip. 0 = never.
            

            

              Change sail to
              

              The new sail angle from that time on.
            

            

              Trip length
              

              How long to sail, up to 60 minutes.

    01 / Build & water
    
            
    
              
            
    
          
    
          
          
    
            
          
    
          
          
    
            +X forward · +Y port · +Z up. Positions in metres from the labelled
            assembly centre. CAD-derived centres are allowed only for uniform
            wooden parts.
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
    
            
    02 / Bottles
    
            
    
              Weigh each bottle with its cap and contents. Balance it across an
              edge to find its balance point.
            
    
            
    
          
    
          
    
            
    03 / Flatpack slats
    
            
    
              Weigh each wooden group with the hardware listed in its name. Each
              physical item belongs to exactly one group. Untick absent groups.
            
    
            
    
          
    
          
    
            
    04 / Control head & sail
    
            
    
              The fixed control housing and the rotating sail unit are weighed
              separately.
            
    
            
    
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
    
            
    05 / Extra loads & ballast
    
            
          
    
          
    
          
    
            Use for equipment or ballast not already included in a group mass.
            For enclosed loads, name the enclosing component. Exposed boxes need
            measured dimensions.
          
    
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
    
            
    06 / Water trials
    
            
          
    
          
    
            Signed millimetres: + above centre, − below. Read the undisturbed
            surface, avoiding the meniscus. Front and rear alone do not
            determine roll. Give every loading a unique configuration name.
